Mold Testing

Scientific Air & Surface Testing in Brockton

Accurate sampling and independent laboratory analysis to identify mold types and spore concentrations within your property.

About This Service

Data-Driven Decisions

Sometimes you need more than a visual inspection to understand what's happening in your home. Professional mold testing provides concrete data about the indoor air quality of your property.

We collect controlled air and surface samples and send them to an independent, accredited laboratory for analysis. The resulting report details exactly what types of mold spores are present and at what concentrations. This scientific approach removes the guesswork and helps determine whether remediation is truly necessary.

Our Process

How Testing Works

1

Strategy Development

We evaluate your property to determine the most effective locations to pull air or take surface samples based on airflow and risk factors.

2

Baseline Sampling

We take an outdoor air sample to establish a natural baseline for your specific neighborhood on that specific day.

3

Indoor Sampling

We use calibrated air pumps to draw specific volumes of indoor air through collection cassettes, capturing airborne spores.

4

Laboratory Analysis

The sealed samples are sent to an independent laboratory under a chain of custody for microscopic analysis by mycologists.

5

Results & Interpretation

Once the lab report is ready (usually 2-3 days), we walk you through the data, explaining what the numbers actually mean for your home.

Questions

Testing FAQ

DIY petri dish kits are notoriously unreliable. They only tell you that mold spores exist in your home — which is true for every home on earth. They don't quantify the air volume, making the results scientifically invalid. Professional testing measures the exact concentration of spores per cubic meter of air.

Standard laboratory turnaround time is typically 2 to 3 business days from the time the samples reach the lab. If you are dealing with a real estate transaction or an urgent health concern, expedited lab processing (24-hour turnaround) is available for an additional fee.

Clearance testing is performed after a mold remediation project is complete, but before the containment barriers are removed. It proves scientifically that the air quality inside the work area has returned to normal, healthy levels and that the remediation was successful.
