Mold Inspection

Thorough Property Assessments in Brockton, MA

We use advanced moisture detection tools to uncover hidden issues, providing you with a clear understanding of your property's condition.

About This Service

Finding What You Can't See

Visual inspections only tell part of the story. Because mold requires moisture to grow, locating the source of hidden moisture is the most critical step in assessing a property.

Our professional mold inspections utilize thermal imaging cameras, pinless moisture meters, and deep structural knowledge to find problems behind walls, under floors, and in attics or crawlspaces. You receive a detailed report with actionable findings, allowing you to make informed decisions about your Brockton home.

Our Process

A Systematic Evaluation

1

Consultation & History

We review the property's history, previous water damage events, and your specific concerns before beginning the physical inspection.

2

Visual Examination

A thorough walkthrough of the property focusing on high-risk areas like basements, attics, bathrooms, and utility rooms.

3

Moisture Detection

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters to detect temperature anomalies and elevated moisture levels behind structural surfaces.

4

Documentation

Extensive photographic documentation of findings, moisture readings, and potential problem areas.

5

Detailed Reporting

We provide a comprehensive written report explaining our findings in plain language, along with recommended next steps.

Inspection FAQ
